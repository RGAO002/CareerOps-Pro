# CareerOps Pro — Design Context

## Target Audience
- Job seekers in North America — Chinese/international professionals and students
- Scenario: optimizing English resumes, matching jobs, preparing interviews
- Pain point: job-seeking anxiety, uncertainty, feeling lost in the process

## Brand Personality & Tone
- **Apple-level polish**: extreme restraint, precision, premium quality feel
- **Claude warmth**: not sterile — approachable, encouraging, human
- **Anxiety-reducing**: users should feel "I'm in good hands"
- **Confident but caring**: authoritative without being cold

## Core Differentiators
1. **Multi-Agent collaboration**: not one AI — a team of specialists that cross-verify
2. **Full pipeline**: resume analysis → editing → cover letter → interview → tracking
3. **Transparent & controllable**: users see AI reasoning and can intervene

## Visual Direction
- **Aesthetic**: Apple product quality + Claude brand color identity
- **Apple DNA**: extreme whitespace, surfaces defined by light not borders, precise typography, quality over quantity
- **Claude DNA**: #DA7756 terracotta/coral as signature accent, warm neutrals
- **Bold contrast**: warm light panels colliding with deep dark tech panels (split compositions)
- **Tech texture**: subtle dot grids, flowing SVG lines, soft glowing orbs — restrained
- **Theme**: Light default, dark mode support planned
- **Layout**: collapsible icon sidebar + top breadcrumb bar
- **Tech stack**: Next.js + shadcn/ui + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion
- **Icons**: Lucide

## Typography
- Display: distinctive serif or editorial font — premium, memorable
- Body: clean geometric sans — readable, modern
- NOT Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, or system defaults

## Color Strategy
- **Primary**: Claude orange (#DA7756 range) — sparingly for maximum impact
- **Backgrounds**: near-white with barely-warm tint, never pure white
- **Dark panels**: deep warm browns/navys for contrast, never pure black
- **Semantic**: sage green (positive), amber (caution), soft red (destructive)
- **Neutrals**: warm-tinted grays, never pure gray

## Emotional Design Principles
- Encouraging language, not grading language
- Progress-oriented: always show forward momentum
- Achievement: positive feedback at every step
- Agents are advisors with personality, not cold bots
- Generous whitespace — room to breathe
- Progressive disclosure — simple first, depth on demand

## Anti-patterns
- NO generic SaaS dashboard aesthetic
- NO purple-blue gradients (AI slop)
- NO pure black (#000) or pure white (#fff)
- NO cards nested in cards
- NO hero metric template (big number + small label)
- NO Inter/Roboto/system fonts
- NO glassmorphism everywhere
- NO monospace as "tech" shorthand
- NOT a data monitoring tool — it's a premium career workspace
